# Complimentary work and VAT – for Mathew to confirm

30 October 2026 (night batch, section 5.7)

## What the ERP does now

- Any free work on a quotation needs a reason: **our mistake / rework**, **goodwill gesture** or **warranty claim**. The owner approves it before the quotation can be sent.
- A job where **everything is free** gets **no tax invoice**. The approved quotation shows the free lines as "NO CHARGE" (our mistake or warranty) or "COMPLIMENTARY (value AED …)" (goodwill), and the job history records "No charge".
- What the free work cost us (labour at the cost rate, parts at cost, film and other materials, recovery) is recorded internally. It counts as a real cost in the daily profit and on the reports. It never counts toward an advisor's bonus.
- A job with **some** free lines is invoiced as usual. The free lines are printed at AED 0 with the same words.

## The question for Mathew

Under UAE VAT, goods or services given away for free in the course of business can be a **deemed supply**, especially goodwill gifts. If a deemed supply applies, output VAT may be due on the cost, and the AED 10,000 per-person yearly gift threshold may matter. Rework of our own faulty work, and warranty repairs, are usually part of the original sale, not a new supply.

Please confirm:

1. For **goodwill** work, do we need to account for output VAT on the cost (a deemed supply), or is no VAT due?
2. For **our mistake / warranty** work, is it right that no new VAT is due?
3. Should a wholly free job still get a **tax invoice at AED 0** for the records?

## How to switch

Settings key `comp_tax_invoice` (Settings, label "Complimentary jobs: issue a tax invoice at AED 0"):

- **Off** (the default) – no tax invoice for a wholly free job.
- **On** – the normal proforma and tax invoice at AED 0 are issued as for any other job.

If goodwill needs output VAT on the cost, tell Omar. The monthly **Complimentary** report (Reports > Complimentary) already lists every free job with its cost to us, by reason, advisor and customer. That is the figure the VAT would be worked out from.
