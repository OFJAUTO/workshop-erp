## PART 1

### U1. The Parts pricing table never moves

- Every row is one grid of fixed-height boxes plus one status line of fixed height. The notes (positions, "Technician wrote", quantity changes, the manager's answer), the "Not finished: …" tag, "Saving…" and errors all live on that status line, truncated with the full text on hover. Nothing appears or disappears that could change a row's height. Checked by measuring every row before and after typing: 78 px each on a wide screen, 118 px each on a 1366 laptop, whatever the row's state.
- The part number stays a box with Copy beside it. It is never swapped for text while the row is open, nor after leaving it.
- Availability is one dropdown on the same line: In stock, Same day, Next day, 2 days, 3 days, Other…, with a small days box beside it that is only visible for Other. Same day saves as ordered (to_order) arriving today, Next day tomorrow, and so on; days are counted in calendar days from today in Dubai. Customers read In stock, Today, Tomorrow, In 3 days on the page and the PDF; Parts read In stock, Same day, Next day, 2 days.
- Rows keep the order the page opened with. A refresh merges what the server sends into the existing order; new rows join at the end; a row being edited keeps the screen's values.
- The "Show only unpriced" filter is a snapshot: the rows shown when it was pressed stay put while they are finished (they turn green in place).
- A quick second save on a brand-new row found the part the first save made (saves run one after another and carry the row's key), so a part can never be made twice.
- Quotation: the parts table has two fixed lines per part (part, then its tags) and a permanent slot for "Below the minimum", so typing a markup never changes a row's height. The inspection checklist, gate-in and QC lists were checked for re-sorting and for boxes swapped to text: none; their remark and parts areas still open on demand by design.

### U2. Number boxes keep what is typed

- One number box for the whole app (`NumericInput`, behind `Input` for every `inputMode="decimal"` or `"numeric"`): while it has the cursor the text is never rewritten ("2.", ".5" and "2,5" stay as typed); about a second after typing stops, or when the box is left, the finished number goes to the rest of the app, dot or comma; on leaving, the box shows the finished number ("2,5" becomes "2.5", ".5" becomes "0.5").
- Hours keep two decimals everywhere: the six hours columns (inspection estimates, job budget, service hours and allowance, work lines) changed from one decimal to two, and the rounding to 0.1 in the quotation, the manager's approval and the technician's estimate was removed.

### U3. One definition of a part's state

- `rowState` in `src/lib/parts-rows.ts` is the one rule: Priced (part number, type, net price above 0, supplier, availability complete) / Not available / Removed / Waiting for owner approval / Not finished. The table on the screen, the counter, the filter, the bottom bar, Pricing done on the server, the advisor's notification, the quotation's blockers and the job card's "x of y parts priced" all read it (the server through `loadPricingRows` in `src/lib/parts-pricing.ts`).
- Pricing done goes straight through when every part is Priced, Not available or Removed. Not available never blocks; the advisor is told with the breakdown ("4 priced, 1 not available"). Only Not finished and Waiting for owner approval hold it: the first press turns those rows red with the missing piece on the status line ("No price", "No supplier", "No availability", "Net price is 0"), the bar names the first one ("1 part not finished · E2e disc: net price is 0") with Go to next (scrolls to the next red row and puts the cursor in the box outlined red) and Send what you have. Finished rows turn green and the count drops. Red appears only after the press.
- The counter reads "15 priced, 1 not available, 1 waiting for owner approval, 2 not finished". A net price of 0 (a discount of 100% or more) never counts as priced; discounts are capped at 100% on save.

## PART 2

1. **Road test one-tap remarks.** Eight items in the owner's order (Noises, Vibration, Pulling to one side, Brakes, Steering, Transmission, Engine, Suspension; the keys of the old five are kept, so saved road tests still read). On Bad or Average, rows of chips (What, Where, When) appear; one tap from each row writes the sentence ("Clunk from the front left over bumps."; vibration "Vibration in the steering wheel above 100 km/h."; the rest "Soft pedal, squeal."), several chips per row allowed, a tapped chip turns light green with a tick and tapping it again rewrites the sentence. The remark stays editable and has spell check. Lists per item in Settings > Inspection checklist > Road test: one-tap remarks; the chips tapped are counted and come first next time.
2. **Tyre brand.** The brand box filters the list as the technician types (Michelin, Pirelli, Continental, Bridgestone, Goodyear, Dunlop, Yokohama, Hankook, Toyo, Falken, Kumho, BFGoodrich, Nexen, Other) and still takes anything typed. An optional Model box sits beside it. "Same as front left" copies brand, model, year, tread and (rear) size. Brands typed on reports that are not on the list are counted at approval and offered on the Inspection checklist settings page with Add / Dismiss. The list is editable in Settings > Inspection.
3. **Positions for paired parts and jobs.** Parts marked Paired in the pool (control arms, bushings, shock absorbers and mounts, air struts and springs, coil springs, stabiliser links and bushings, tie rod ends, wheel bearings, CV boots, brake discs, headlights) show big buttons Front pair, Rear pair, All four (quantity 2, 2, 4) and a small "One side only…" that reveals the four corners. Brake pads stay per axle set, Front / Rear. Lists read "Lower control arm - front pair - 2"; the quotation, PDF and Parts page say "front pair", "rear pair", "all four". Labour lines offer Front pair, Rear pair and All four too. Paired is a toggle per part in Settings > Parts list.
4. **Several parts for one finding.** The picker already stayed open for several taps with one Done; it now says so, and the button under an item with parts reads "+ Add another part".
5. **Goes with.** Each pool part can name the parts offered alongside (Settings > Parts list, "Goes with"): brake pads and brake discs offer the brake pad wear sensor for that axle (the sensor is a per-axle part, Front / Rear, never a checklist item); water pump offers gasket and coolant; engine oil and oil filter offer the drain plug and washer; spark plugs offer ignition coils; the A/C compressor offers compressor oil and the receiver drier. The offers appear in the picker, under the gathered parts list (technician and manager) and under the quotation's parts block ("+ Ask Parts for front brake pad wear sensor?"). Nothing is ever added by itself.
6. **No required remark where the choices describe the item.** OK / Required items, brake discs with a condition, leaks with a severity or repair, fluids with a quantity, brake pads with the measured thickness, the battery with its result and the tyres with their answers need no written remark: the system writes it from the choices ("Close to minimum, skimming possible"), shows it as a line and keeps the box folded behind "Add a remark" / "Edit the remark". Plain Good / Average / Bad items still require the remark.
7. **One-tap reasons.** A shared `ReasonChips` component: chips above the box, one tap writes the reason, several allowed (joined with "; "), green when selected, typing always possible. Wired to the manager's hours change (raise and lower lists, only the direction chosen shows), sending a report back, pausing and leaving a job, the keys override and the release at gate-out, the LPO deposit override, the owner's discount on a part, voiding a receipt, "Remove: not needed", the special move request and decision, and the credit note request. Every list is editable in Settings > One-tap reasons. The browser's "Please fill out this field" bubble never shows: the refused box gets a red outline and the system's message under it ("Please fill this in", "Please choose one", "Please tick this", "Please enter a number between …"); the first one gets the cursor.
8. **Recovery: who pays.** The row starts with Who pays? Us / Customer. Us: type, trips, cost per trip, provider (our recovery or an external company); no price box; the line is off the customer's quotation and its cost sits in the internal breakdown; the tick "Mention to the customer as free" shows "Recovery, closed box, two ways: free of charge" at AED 0 on the page and the PDF. Customer: as before, with the price per trip. Show / Hide is gone.
9. **Documents not sent.** On each job card and dashboard line: grey while waiting, amber "Report not sent · 25 min" after the first limit, red after the second (with a reminder to the advisor from the hourly job, once per document), green "Report sent 14:05" once sent; then for the customer "Sent, not opened · 5 h" and "Opened, no reply · 2 h". Watched: the inspection report after approval (15 / 30 min), the quotation after Pricing done (1 h / 2 h), the proforma after the car is ready (30 min / 1 h), the tax invoice after the payment that made it (15 / 30 min), the customer's reply (4 h / next working day). Working time only. The dashboard's Pending row has a "Not sent" count. Limits in Settings > Documents not sent.
10. **Remove: not needed.** In the Parts row menu, separate from Not available, with the one-tap reasons Duplicate / Already covered by another part / Technician mistake / Not required for this job / Other. Before the order: the part comes off the list and the quotation, stays greyed and struck through with "Removed by <name>: <reason>", Undo for a few seconds then Put back; the advisor and the workshop manager are told; a sent quotation is flagged; removed parts leave the pricing count. A request without a part can be removed the same way. After the order: the same menu item leads to the three endings (return, stock, fit with approval).
11. **Supplier standard discount and limits.** Parts, accounts and the owner set each supplier's standard discount (Gargash starts at 55%); every change is logged with who and when (`supplier_discount_changes`). Choosing a supplier fills the standard; a changed line notes "Standard 55%, changed to 60%". Changing a standard applies to new and unpriced lines by itself; for priced lines on open jobs the Suppliers page asks "Apply 60% to the 6 open Gargash lines?" and applies it in one tap (sent quotations are flagged). A discount above the owner's limit (Settings > Parts, 70%) turns the row amber "Waiting for owner approval", tells the owner with part, supplier, list price, discount and net, and waits; the owner approves or refuses with one tap on /parts/approvals (refused goes back to the standard). A discount can never exceed 100%; a net of 0 never counts as priced.
12. **Unfinished parts in red with a jump button.** Built with U3 above.

## Assumptions

- Hours take two decimals now ("10.25" stays 10.25); the 0.1 steps of the − and + buttons are unchanged.
- The parts markup box keeps the minimum markup rule (5% today): a figure below it is raised to the minimum on leaving the box, so the persistence checks for that box use 10.25 and 12,5.
- "Same day" is to_order with the delivery date today, so it goes on an LPO and counts as here today for planning.
- The "Show only unpriced" filter shows what was unpriced when it was pressed and does not hide a row that gets finished while it is open.
- The recovery line defaults to "Customer" pays, as before, until Us is tapped.
- The removed-request state uses the request's existing "rejected" status plus the removal columns; "Cannot source" and "Already covered" remain hidden as before.
- The owner's approvals page is reached from the notification and from the Parts desk is not linked yet; the limit lives in Settings > Parts.
- Document reminders count working time from Settings (opening and closing hours, working days, breaks).
