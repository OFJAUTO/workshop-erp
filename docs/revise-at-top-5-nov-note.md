# Revise at the top + profit with no labour cost — 5 November 2026

The real case: **E-00056** (painting exterior trims). Its profit read 97.7 % on v1 (87.2 % on v2): no labour was
counted at all.

## The cause (one line)

E-00056's two lines are typed fixed-price lines, and the hours box was only shown on bodyshop services from the
list, so nobody could type their hours and the one shared calculation found no labour to cost.

## What was built

- **Revise at the top.** One black **"Revise → version 2"** (3, 4 …) in the header row next to Preview PDF, on
  estimates and on job quotations alike, whenever the document was sent, opened or approved (an accepted estimate
  that is not on a job yet too). The bottom Revise box on the estimate page and the Revise in the quotation's bottom
  bar are gone: **one place**. Owner and advisors only (on estimates: the owner and the advisor in charge). On a
  quotation the customer approved, an advisor's same button asks the owner first (the revision rules are unchanged),
  and arriving from "Parts changed after approval" it opens already filled in.
- **Versions by the title**: "E-00056 · Estimate · **v1 | v2**". Each opens; the one on screen is marked; an older
  version is read-only and has no Revise (only the newest can be revised). The new version is a copy of the last one;
  the customer's link shows the newest sent version; an approved document needs the customer's approval again.
- **Hours on every fixed-price line of our own work**: the "our hours" box (internal, never on the customer's
  documents) now shows on every typed or listed fixed-price line, not only bodyshop services. It is red while empty.
  The profit counts **hours × AED 50** (the cost rate in Settings) through the one shared calculation. Estimates now
  have the **Materials** box too (paint, film…).
- **"Hours missing – profit not reliable"** in red next to the profit — in the internal box (with the lines that
  have no hours) and on the profit chip in the bottom bar — while any line of our own work has no hours. The profit is
  **never green** then. The same on job quotations, including approved (locked) ones.
- Once the technicians' clocked hours cost the labour (work done), labour lines count as costed; typed fixed-price
  lines still need their hours.

## Assumptions

- "Labour cost" for a fixed-price line = its "our hours" × the cost rate in Settings (AED 50 on live today), the
  same figure the internal box, the owner's reports and the advisors' target already use.
- The "Declined: what next?" card keeps its two big choices (Revise / Proceed to gate-out): it is the decision after
  a decline, not a second revise box.
- A draft has no Revise (there is nothing to revise until it goes out); an older version has none either (only the
  newest can be revised).
