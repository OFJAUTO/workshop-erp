# Tax invoices and credit notes on the new document layout – for Mathew to confirm

31 October 2026

All customer documents now follow the owner's mockup. Every amount is printed **once**:

- the package price only beside the package;
- a section subtotal only when it adds something;
- one total, "TOTAL INCL. VAT".

On a **tax invoice** and a **credit note**, the ERP still prints these fields every time, even when one of them repeats a figure:

- the supplier's name, address and **TRN** (in the header);
- **"TAX INVOICE"** or **"CREDIT NOTE"** as the title, with the **number** and the **date of issue**;
- the customer's name, address details and **TRN** (when the customer has one);
- each line's description, quantity and amount;
- **"Subtotal before VAT"**. This is the **taxable amount**. It is always printed on these two documents, even with a single line. On a quotation or estimate with a single line it is left out.
- **"VAT 5%"**, the VAT amount;
- **"TOTAL INCL. VAT"**, the total including VAT;
- **"AED"** on the totals.

A credit note also says that it corrects an earlier tax invoice, and its total reads "CREDIT TOTAL INCL. VAT".

Payments recorded against the invoice appear inside the same box as "Paid … (RCT-…)", followed by "BALANCE DUE". These are different amounts, so they are not repeats.
