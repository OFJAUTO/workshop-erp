# Part labels 50 × 25 mm, complete – 6 November 2026 (Section 8)

Live, no database change. Merges into the 50 × 25 layout of 5 November.

- **The label PDF:** every page is exactly the label (50 × 25 mm, the size in Settings > Stickers), zero margins, one label per page – the printer cannot scale it wrongly.
- **On the label:** a 21 mm QR (opens the part), the job number, the plate (or STOCK), the part with its side, the make and model, the LPO number with "1 OF 2", and the day it was received. No prices, no customer.
- **One label per piece** ("1 OF 2", "2 OF 2"); a fluid in litres is one label. **Fewer:** untick on the label sheet, then **PDF of the selected**.
- **Where:** receiving (opens by itself as before), **Parts arrivals → Print today's labels**, the **LPO page → Print the QR labels** (that LPO's parts) or **Pick which labels**, a part's **⋯ → Print the QR label**, and the job's label sheet – any time.
- **Test label:** Settings > Stickers → **Test label PDF (with its outline)**, and on the label sheet.
- **Peter's one-page guide:** `docs/part-labels-guide-for-peter.md` (Windows printer settings 50 × 25, Chrome print settings, the test label, calibrating the ESLER ES-BTT41). Nothing is published for that exact model, so the calibration steps are the usual feed-button ones with "follow the printer's own card if it differs".
