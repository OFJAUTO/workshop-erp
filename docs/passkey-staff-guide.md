# Signing in with your phone (passkey)

**For:** the owner, service advisors, parts, gate-in, accounts, QC and the workshop manager.
The bodyshop manager, team leaders and technicians: nothing changes for you.

## What changes

- You sign in with **your phone** instead of typing a password. Your phone's Face ID or fingerprint is the key.
- Your **password stays as a backup**, for days you don't have your phone. Each time you use it, the owner is told.
- You stay signed in for **12 hours**, then you sign in again (a new sign-in each morning).
- If you also had a tablet PIN: once your phone is set up, the PIN **no longer signs you in**. It still signs for parts.

## Before you start

- An iPhone or an Android phone with Face ID, a fingerprint or a screen lock switched on.
- At an office computer: **Bluetooth must be ON** on the computer and on the phone, and the phone needs internet.

## The first time (about one minute)

1. Sign in with your email and password as usual. The ERP shows **"Set up your phone to sign in"**.
2. **At a computer:** press **"Use my phone (scan a QR code)"**. Point your phone's camera at the QR code, tap the link that appears, and approve with Face ID or your fingerprint. If the phone asks to save a passkey, press **Continue**.
   **On a phone or tablet:** press **"Use this phone or tablet"** and approve with Face ID or your fingerprint.
3. You see **"Done ✓"**. That's all.

**Android:** when your phone asks whether to remember the computer, say **yes**. Next time the computer sends the question straight to your phone, with no QR code.
**iPhone:** the computer shows the QR code every time: scan it and approve (about 10 seconds).

## Every day after that

1. Open the ERP and press **"Sign in with passkey"**.
2. **Computer:** approve the question on your phone (Android), or scan the QR code (iPhone). **Phone or tablet:** Face ID or fingerprint.
3. You're in for 12 hours.

## A second phone

Menu → tap your name at the bottom → **My sign-in** → **"Add another phone"**. You approve with your first phone, then set up the new one.

## Lost or changed your phone?

1. **Tell the owner straight away** (call or WhatsApp). Only the owner can remove a phone.
2. Until then, sign in with your email and password (**"Or use your password (backup)"**).
3. When the owner has removed the old phone, the ERP asks you to set up your new phone at your next sign-in.

Forgot your password too? Ask the owner for a new setup link.

## Choosing a password (when the ERP asks)

At least **10 characters**, with a **small letter**, a **capital letter** and a **number**. Symbols are allowed but not needed. Passwords that have leaked from other websites are refused.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Cancelled or timed out"**: press the button again.
- **The computer says "Connecting…" and nothing happens** (after 30 seconds the ERP says so): your phone and the computer can't find each other.
  - Check **Bluetooth is on, on both**, and keep the phone **close to the computer**.
  - Or set up the passkey **on your phone** instead: open **erp.ofjauto.com** on your phone, sign in, add passkey. From then on, sign in on your phone or approve from it.
- **Your phone needs a screen lock** (PIN, Face ID or fingerprint), or it cannot keep a passkey.
- **Where the passkey is kept**:
  - **iPhone**: iCloud Keychain (Settings › your name › iCloud › Passwords and Keychain: on).
  - **Android**: Google Password Manager, or Samsung Pass on a Samsung phone.
- **Shared device**: never save your passkey on an office PC or a workshop tablet. The ERP warns you: save it on your own phone instead.
- **Lost your phone?** Ask the owner for a **one-time sign-in code** (it works once, for 15 minutes). On the login page press "Lost your phone? Use the owner's one-time code", type your email and the code, then set up your new phone. Nobody else can reset it for you.
- **"This phone was removed"**: the owner removed it. Sign in with your password, then set up your phone again.
- Still stuck? Ask the owner.

**Never approve a sign-in on your phone that you did not start yourself.**
