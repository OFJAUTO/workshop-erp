# Printing part labels (50 × 25 mm) – for Peter

**What a label shows:** the QR (scan it with a phone: it opens the part), the job number and the plate (or STOCK), the part with its side, the make and model, the LPO number with "1 OF 2", and the day it arrived. No prices, no customer.

## Where to print from
- **Receiving:** when you tick parts received, the labels open by themselves.
- **Parts arrivals:** **Print today's labels** (top right) – every part received today.
- **An LPO:** **Print the QR labels** (all of that LPO) or **Pick which labels**.
- **One part:** the part's **⋯** menu → **Print the QR label**. Any time, as often as needed.
- **Fewer labels:** open the label sheet, untick the ones you don't want, press **PDF of the selected**.

## Set the printer up once (Windows)
1. Start → Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Printers & scanners → **ESLER ES-BTT41** → **Printing preferences**.
2. **Paper size:** choose **50 × 25 mm**. If it isn't in the list, press **New / Custom**, type width **50 mm**, height **25 mm**, name it "OFJ label", save, then pick it.
3. **Media / paper type:** labels with gaps. Print speed: medium. Darkness: middle (raise it if the QR prints grey).
4. Press **OK**.

## Print (Chrome)
1. The label PDF opens in a new tab. Press **Ctrl + P**.
2. **Printer:** ESLER ES-BTT41. **Paper size:** 50 × 25 mm.
3. **Scale:** 100% / **Actual size** (never "Fit"). **Margins:** None. **Headers and footers:** off.
4. **Print.** One page = one label.

## Test label
Settings → Stickers → **Test label PDF (with its outline)** (also on the label sheet). The outline should sit evenly inside the label on all four sides.
- Outline cut off at the top or bottom → calibrate (below).
- Everything slightly small or large → Scale was not 100%.
- Label printed sideways → in Printing preferences switch **Orientation** (Portrait ↔ Landscape) and test again.

## Calibrate the printer (when labels drift or skip)
1. Load the roll, close the lid, printer **on**.
2. **Hold the Feed button** until the light flashes / the printer beeps, then let go. It feeds a few labels and measures the gaps.
3. Press **Feed once**: exactly one label should come out and stop at the tear edge.
4. If that doesn't work: turn the printer **off**, hold **Feed**, turn it **on**, let go after the beeps.

The ESLER's own card or manual has the exact light pattern for this model; if it says something different, follow it.
